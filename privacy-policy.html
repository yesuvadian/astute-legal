Privacy Policy

Astute Society · Effective 24 November 2026

Astute Society ("the app", "we", "us") helps residential communities and housing societies manage maintenance billing, collections, expenses, bank reconciliation and accounts. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the Astute Society Android app and web app.

1. Who is responsible

The app is provided by Astute, Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. Each community (for example, a housing society's managing committee) decides what resident and financial information it enters into the app and who in the community can see it. For that information the community acts as the data owner, and we process it on the community's behalf to provide the service.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, call logs or device identifiers, and the app contains no advertising or third-party analytics or tracking.

3. Device permissions

  • Internet — to reach the community's records on our servers.
  • Phone dialer — when you tap a mobile number, the app opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in. The app cannot place calls by itself and does not read your call history.
  • Files and sharing — only files you pick are read, and bills or receipts are shared only through the share sheet when you choose to share them.

4. How information is shared

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. Information is shared only:

  • within the community, with members whose role allows them to see it (for example, the treasurer sees dues and receipts);
  • with service providers that host and operate the app for us, such as our cloud database and authentication provider (Supabase), under contracts that require them to protect it;
  • when required by law, court order or to protect the rights and safety of users or others.

5. Storage and security

Information is stored with our cloud hosting provider and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access is limited by community and by role, every financial change is recorded in an audit history, and accounting records cannot be silently altered once posted. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify affected communities of any breach as required by law.

6. How long we keep information

We keep a community's information for as long as the community uses the service. Financial and accounting records may need to be kept for the period required by applicable Indian law (for example, tax and audit rules) even after an account is closed. After that, information is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. Because resident and financial records belong to your community, many requests are best made to your community's committee or administrator, who can update records directly in the app. You can also contact us (section 10) and we will help or pass your request to the community.

8. Deleting your account

To delete your Astute Society account, email us from the address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account", or ask your community administrator to deactivate it. We will remove your sign-in and personal profile within 30 days. Entries already posted in a community's accounts (for example, a receipt in your name) may be retained as described in section 6, because the community is required to keep them.

9. Children

The app is intended for adults managing community finances and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly create accounts for children.
